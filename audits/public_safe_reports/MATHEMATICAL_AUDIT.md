# Mathematical review

The Fourier transform of the radial autocorrelation is a square. Its weighted radial integral is one, and its pairing with the Bessel basis picks out the dilated multiplier. The asymptotics yield normalization comparable to the square root of the mode index. The dyadic mass sequence is summable. For each block its designated bump alone supplies the prescribed lower bound, while the terms from larger and smaller scales obey the stated geometric envelopes. Both envelopes tend to zero against the null block maxima.

The Dirichlet heat semigroup preserves radiality and contracts the weighted L¹ norm. Pairing its spectral expansion with the constant function gives the alternating-sign Bessel integrals. Bounded truncations and fixed-time exponential damping justify the formula for L¹ input. A prescribed single-parity positive tail would force a signed heat pairing to become unbounded, which is impossible.
