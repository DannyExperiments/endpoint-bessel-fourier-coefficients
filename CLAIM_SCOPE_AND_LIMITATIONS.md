# Claim scope and limitations

- The construction concerns order-zero Bessel–Fourier coefficients with the source normalization, the weighted space $L^1(x\,dx)$, and every nonnegative null sequence.
- The constructed function is nonnegative and supported in $[0,1/2]$. Its normalized coefficients converge to zero while majorizing the given sequence.
- The cited modified Fejér kernel counterexamples are earlier work. The proof uses a different kernel.
- The parity obstruction concerns a prescribed positive lower bound on one parity while the other vanishes. General divergence of unregularized partial sums remains outside scope.
- Python and Lean check the dyadic arithmetic and a scalar comparison for the illustrative disk. The analytical coefficient estimates and heat contradiction use the written proof.
